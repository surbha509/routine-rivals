Keep in mind

Your progress is calculated from active tasks scheduled for each day. A day with no scheduled tasks neither counts as a successful day nor breaks or extends a streak. Today's unfinished tasks do not reset a streak before the day ends. The current weekly leaderboard remains provisional through Sunday. The previous week is shown separately; historical results are recalculated, not locked snapshots; ties are broken by successful days, current streak, then weekly completion. Keep your schedule realistic so you can build a routine that lasts.
